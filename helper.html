Right-click any question. Tutorly explains it.

The free Tutorly Helper adds “Ask Tutorly” to your browser’s right-click menu. The question opens in the Tutorly app with the answer and every step.

Select textHighlight a question, right-click, and choose Ask Tutorly.
Right-click a pictureA diagram, graph or screenshot of a problem.
Snip part of a pageClick the Tutorly button or press Alt+Shift+T, then drag a box.

Install it (Chrome or Microsoft Edge, about a minute)

You need the Tutorly app on the same computer. Get it on the home page.

Download Tutorly Helper
  1. Open the downloaded tutorly-helper.zip and click Extract all. Keep the extracted folder somewhere it won't be deleted, like Documents.
  2. In your browser’s address bar, go to chrome://extensions (Chrome) or edge://extensions (Edge).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted tutorly-helper folder.
  5. Done. Right-click any question on a page and choose Ask Tutorly. Pin the Tutorly button from the puzzle-piece menu for quick snips.
Coming soon: one-click install from the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons, without Developer mode.

The helper only talks to the Tutorly app on your own computer (127.0.0.1). It doesn’t read pages on its own, track what you browse, or send anything to the internet. It only acts when you right-click and choose Ask Tutorly, or click its button.
